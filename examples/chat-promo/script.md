# Bliz Studio sample promo: script v4 draft (supermarket story, 2026-11-20)

Story from the user: a small supermarket sees another store's website, decides it wants its own, hears about Bliz Studio,
and messages Bliz with screenshots in hand. Bliz answers: no worries, we've got you covered.
Format: 1080x1920, about 21.9 s, no voiceover. On-screen text and chats only; sound effects; no music.
No opening logo.

All people, shops and websites in the story are invented. The other store's website has no name or logo, so it
cannot be mistaken for a real business.

| Time | Scene | What we see |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0-3.0 s | 1. The scroll | Straight in on a phone, late at night ("11:47 PM"). A thumb-scroll through a clean supermarket website: "Groceries delivered to your door", product tiles, "Order now". Small label above the phone: "Mama T's Supermarket, after closing." |
| 3.0-8.5 s | 2. The idea (chat: "Tobi, Manager") | You send a screenshot of that site: "See this supermarket's website." Tobi: "We need one of our own o." You: "Who will even build it?" Tobi: "Ask Bliz Studio. They do websites." |
| 8.5-10.0 s | 3. Card | "So we asked." |
| 10.0-16.8 s | 4. The enquiry (chat: "Bliz Studio", B symbol as the avatar) | You: "Hello! We run Mama T's Supermarket." You send two screenshots. You: "Can we get something like this?" Bliz typing. Bliz: "No wahala, we've got you covered." Bliz: "Let's build one that's all yours." Push-in on Bliz's reply. |
| 16.8-18.8 s | 5. Card | "Your business. Your website." (second sentence in yellow) |
| 18.8-21.3 s | 6. End | Bliz lockup. "Ideas. In motion." Button: "DM CREATE to work with us". |

Why it is built this way:
- The want comes from the shop itself (seeing what others have), not from a problem we invent for them.
- Screenshots in hand is how many owners really brief a studio; showing them makes the enquiry feel real.
- Bliz's reply turns "something like this" into "one that's all yours": Bliz builds original work, not copies.
- The last card repeats the outcome in four words, then one action.
- Brand manual: outcome first, short sentences, Nigerian context where natural ("o", "No wahala"), humour never at a
  customer's expense, one call to action, no invented claims. "Ideas. In motion." and "DM CREATE to work with us" are
  the manual's own copy.

Open: shop and person names, the Bliz reply wording, whether to keep "DM CREATE" (it needs the verified handle on screen).
